Expense Tracking

Categorize every expense — canteen, metro, textbooks, hostel rent, entertainment — in seconds. Just paste your UPI SMS.

Budget Management

Set daily limits (e.g., ₹350/day) and monthly caps. Color-coded progress bars show your safe zone at a glance.

Savings Goals

Dream of a MacBook, a Goa trip, or a coding bootcamp? Create a goal, set a deadline, and deposit savings with one tap.

Smart Alerts

Get visual warnings at 80%, 90%, and 100% of your daily and monthly budget — before you overspend.

Expense Analytics

Interactive charts reveal where your money actually goes — food, travel, subscriptions, or books. Stop guessing.

Budget Forecasting

Based on your burn rate, we predict exactly how much you'll have at month-end — so you never get caught short.

UPI Statement Reader

Paste your bank SMS.
We do the rest.

Copy any UPI transaction SMS from Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, SBI, HDFC, ICICI, or any Indian bank — and our AI engine automatically detects the amount, merchant, category, and payment app.

  • Swiggy, Zomato → Food & Canteen
  • Metro, Rapido, Uber → Travel
  • Udemy, Hostel, Xerox → Education / Hostel
  • Scholarship credit → Income

Our Story

Built by students, for students.

CampusSpend was born out of a simple frustration: pocket money disappears, scholarships vanish on random expenses, and by end of the month there's nothing left for the things that matter.

We built a tool that understands Indian student life — chai tapri runs, metro commutes, hostel mess bills, and semester textbook expenses. Everything tracked automatically from your UPI apps.
